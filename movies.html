Stories I Enjoy Watching

I usually choose something to watch when I have free time or feel bored. I am drawn to stories that have suspense, unusual characters or a twist I did not see coming.

  1. Locke & Key – I enjoy its mystery, secrets and unexpected developments.
  2. From – Its suspense and unanswered questions make it engaging.
  3. School for Good and Evil – I like its fantasy setting and twists around good and evil.
  4. Young Sheldon – I enjoy its humour and family-centred storytelling.
  5. The Big Bang Theory – Its comedy and unusual characters make it entertaining.
